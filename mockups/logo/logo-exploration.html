Praxis — Logo Exploration

Five logo concepts for the Praxis math practice platform. Each concept is shown on light, dark, and white backgrounds, plus at favicon and hero sizes. All logos are inline SVG.

Design Research — Key Insights

  • The "x" is the asset. Praxis has a natural geometric centerpiece: the letter X. It's a cross, a variable, a multiplication sign, a coordinate axis intersection. Every strong concept should leverage this.
  • Educational logos skew geometric. Brilliant uses a geometric star. Wolfram uses angular shapes. Desmos is a clean wordmark. The pattern: mathematical precision rendered as visual design.
  • Short names work as wordmarks. At 6 letters, "Praxis" is compact enough for a pure typographic treatment. The X provides natural visual weight.
  • Scalability test: favicon. The standalone mark must read at 16px. Complex shapes fail here. Simple geometry wins.
  • Color role: Teal = primary/trust/scholarship. Orange = accent/energy/action. The logo should be primarily teal with orange as an optional accent.
  • Avoid: Pi symbols (overused), graduation caps (generic), lightbulbs (cliché), golden spirals (overused in math branding).

A. Praxis Axis

The X in Praxis is rendered as a coordinate axis cross — two perpendicular lines that evoke both graph paper and the mathematical variable x. The horizontal bar extends slightly beyond the letter, suggesting forward momentum. The wordmark uses geometric letter spacing with the X as the visual anchor point. Bold, modern, mathematical.

B. Praxis Scholar

A refined wordmark where the X is drawn with two intersecting pen strokes — evoking handwriting, the act of working through problems on paper. The strokes are slightly calligraphic, contrasting with the geometric sans-serif of the other letters. Scholarly yet human. The standalone mark is the penned X alone.

C. Praxis Grid

A combination mark: the standalone icon is an X inscribed within a rounded square — like a checkbox being marked, or a point on graph paper. The square suggests structure (a test, a worksheet, a grid), while the X suggests completion and action. The word "Praxis" sits beside it in clean weight 700. Practical, structured, satisfying.

D. Praxis Iterate

The standalone mark is three progressively bolder X strokes layered concentrically — each iteration slightly larger and darker than the last, visualizing the concept of "practice makes progress." The lightest X is the first attempt, the boldest is mastery. The word "Praxis" uses extra-bold weight with tight spacing. Energetic, growth-oriented.

E. Praxis Pure

A pure wordmark — no icon, no mark, just the word "Praxis" in Plus Jakarta Sans Extra Bold with the X highlighted in the accent orange. The simplest possible logo. The orange X is the brand mark when extracted. Relies entirely on typography and color. Confident, minimal, Desmos-like clarity. The favicon is just a teal "P" or the orange "X".
